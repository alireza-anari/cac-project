## Executive disposition

**ACCEPT the bounded S0-09H real-quorum experiment and its explicit limitations as useful architecture evidence only. DO NOT ratify the full Lane2 anti-rollback recovery/security protocol, the root's own majority-rollback safety, or Lane3 orphan recovery.** The tested supported scope was a **current 3-of-5 non-restored and admitted etcd R majority**, under a trusted single-host controller/PG observer and logical W/application restores. An independently enforced current authorization admission boundary was exercised in the experimental test harness; physical/administrative independence of that boundary is unproven.

The experiment **does add evidence beyond** the earlier S0-09F assumed-W oracle: it uses actual five-member etcd 3.6.16 processes, live quorum/current reads, atomic root state/journal CAS, member identity and voting state, restored W and actual nonvoting fresh-learner replacements with full replay, real guarded PostgreSQL and PgBouncer, actual process crash and lost-reply cuts. These are still same-host synthetic fault schedules, **not** Production failure-domain independence, formal Raft correctness proof or general physical disaster recovery.

The original S0-09D C-only anchor epoch2-to-epoch1 rollback **remains `FAIL_SECURITY`** (prior A revived and obtained fresh issuance/scoped RLS). That original safety assertion remains unchanged. The H-path conditional old-W restore denial must never be described as repairing or reclassifying that earlier failing mechanism.

### Accepted observed slices, bounded by the test model

- Real five-voter current majority and atomic root owner/epoch/policy/OPEN state with contiguous journal and authenticated current reads; limited actual W logical e2→e1 restore against R e2/B, prior A denied fresh issue/SQL/RLS and B recovered after full replay.
- Combined *missing exact live OPEN in restored W* plus **fresh r4/r5 nonvoting learner replacement/catch-up** while the surviving r1/r2/r3 current committed head and OPEN remained intact. This is **NOT** a literal stale Raft WAL/snapshot import into an existing voter or learner.
- Guard/manifest, private legacy ACL and SQL/RLS gates in supported routes, actual size-one PgBouncer PID reused with new full transaction IDs and hostile old session state denied; cancellation retained UNKNOWN and successor was excluded.
- Total order of FIRST_USE_GRANTED and REVOKE_INTENT for tested cases; old committed work may complete while successor stays NOT_READY until known durable CLOSE. Eleven genuine worker SIGKILL cuts included pre-ABORT and post-positive-ABORT/pre-CLOSE; actual lost Root OPEN/CLOSE/issuer replies and proxy-withheld PG COMMIT/ABORT terminal replies had conservative outcomes. No unproved unknown outcome was automatically released.
- Five isolated mutation probes: (1) current-head **readiness-only** stale acceptance (with real current ADMIT still denying old actor—not a demonstrated RLS bypass), (2) current guard admission, (3) complete W replay, (4) first-use vs revoke, (5) unresolved hold exclusion. Each deliberately broke its intended original assertion, then was restored and passed; no Source/R journal “time travel” was used as cleanup.
- Actual signer retirement tested by Root CAS, but **policy retirement was NOT_RUN**. A known-container artifact credential scanner was fixed and tested with two nonsecurity-suite methods; it is not complete DLP or external custody proof.

Even a current guard cannot detect a convincingly rewound majority without an independently retained newer fact. |

| **P1 / operational unknown** | One-host controlled failure domain, trusted controller and PG outcome observer, catalog attestation not universally complete, journal compaction/retention growth, sustained close-contention/latency/capacity/cost, full independent verifier/root bootstrap, operational key/certificate lifecycle. |
